Built for teaching.
Studied with care.

MISRA is a local senior-project prototype exploring human–AI grading agreement and the value of retrieving approved course material.

Course retrieval uses bounded BM25 over approved passages. Evaluation compares proposed grades with independent instructor marks and examines what course evidence contributes.

Model confidence is uncalibrated. Citations and successful software checks do not establish grading accuracy.

A few things to know.

Who decides the final grade?

The instructor. MISRA proposes marks and explanations; instructors review, adjust and save their decisions.

Can I use Arabic and English?

The instructor interface supports Arabic, English and right-to-left layouts. Extracted answers stay in their original language for review.

Do I need to understand the AI settings?

Your workflow is the marking guide, papers, review and feedback. Model configuration is handled separately from the instructor’s marking task.

Where does processing happen?

This prototype runs locally, with background jobs and configured AI services. Those services may receive exam content. Use synthetic or redacted material for demonstrations.
